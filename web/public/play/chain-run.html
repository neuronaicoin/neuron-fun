Pick your chain

Four chains race to the finish. Run in your chain's lane and grab what pushes it forward. Every other lane helps a rival.

← → change lane · ↑ / space jump · phone: touch a lane, swipe up to jump

How it plays
  • You back one chain. Its runner is on the race bar at the top, next to three rivals.
  • A coin you grab pushes the runner of the lane you're in. Stay in your lane to help your chain, or slip into a rival's lane to dodge, at a price.
  • A red candle knocks that lane's runner back. A rug hole trips the runner for a moment. Jump both.
  • The crowd buys too: rivals move on their own, and now and then a whale pushes one forward.
  • First runner over the line wins. No score, no points, just which chain took it.
