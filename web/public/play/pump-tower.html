Pump your chain

1Pick a chain
2Tap as fast as you can

How it plays
  • Pick a chain. Its pool is the tower marked YOU.
  • Every tap is a buy: your pool grows.
  • Sometimes your pool flashes red: a dump is coming. Tapping while it's red loses you blocks, so hold off for a second.
  • The other chains grow on their own, and a whale sometimes lands a big buy on one of them.
  • After 30 seconds the tallest pool wins. The other three pour into it and it locks, just like the end of a real sasa race.
